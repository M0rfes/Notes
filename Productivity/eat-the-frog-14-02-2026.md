# Eat That Frog!

> "If you have to eat two frogs, eat the biggest one first."

### Core Philosophy
Hoping to finish all your tasks is unrealistic. We will never finish everything; there is never enough time. Therefore, we must focus on the most essential tasks. The rest of the low-priority tasks can wait or be delegated.

### Visualize Success
See yourself where you want to be. This help you find people already there; ask them for advice or study their path to emulation.

---

## The 7-Step Plan for Success

1. **Decide what you want**: Balance life and career goals. Knowing what we want is the first step.
2. **Write it down**: A goal not written down is just a wish. Writing makes it real.
3. **Set a Deadline**: Break goals into milestones with specific deadlines to create urgency.
4. **Make a List**: List every skill and task required to reach your milestone.
5. **Make a Plan**: Organize the list into a roadmap. Prioritize high-impact tasks and blockers.
6. **Take Action**: An average plan executed is better than a perfect strategy that never starts.
7. **Do something each day**: Maintain momentum. Don't let procrastination set in as the TODO list grows.

---

## Planning and Focus

### Proper Prior Planning Prevents Poor Performance
Plan every Day, Week, Month, and Year.
- **Master List**: All items for the year.
- **Monthly Plan**: What needs to be done this month.
- **Weekly Plan**: Set aside 2 hours every Sunday.
- **Daily Plan**: Plan every night for the upcoming day.

### Focus on Activities, Not Just Accomplishments
Busywork is not the same as meaningful work. 
- **[[80/20 Rule]]**: 80% of success comes from 20% of the most important tasks.
- **Consequences**: Consider the long-term impact of doing (or not doing) a task. Focus on core skills that give a strategic advantage.

### Law of Forced Efficiency
> "There is never enough time to do everything, but there is always sufficient time to focus on what is the most important thing."

Add an extra **20% buffer** for errors and rework. Always ask:
- *What are my highest-value activities?*
- *What is something only I can do that will make a real difference?*
