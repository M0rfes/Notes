# Financial Statements Overview

These folders hold all financial statements in PDF format. 

## Goals
- **Archive:** Store older PDF statements for historical records.
- **Correlation:** Match expenses from PDFs with records in [[Daily Notes]]. 
    - *Note:* Older expenses might not be fully reflected in current notes.
- **Pattern Recognition:** Identify spending patterns and record where the most significant spending occurs.
- **Optimization:** Get the most utility out of financial data.
