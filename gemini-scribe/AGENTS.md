## Vault Overview

This vault is a comprehensive technical knowledge base and workspace centered on Large Language Models (LLMs), AI systems engineering (specifically the Gemini ecosystem), and robust software architecture with a heavy focus on Domain-Driven Design (DDD).

It functions as both a deep-dive research repository and a production environment for professional content creation. The vault balances theoretical study with practical implementation details across approximately 60 specialized notes, showing a significant recent expansion in architectural patterns and social media drafting.

## Organization

The vault is organized into functional domains with a clear emphasis on categorization and temporal tracking:

- **Technical Repositories**: Specialized research is housed in folders like `LLMs/` (dense research on RAG and caching), `Architecture/` (now including sub-directories for `DDD` and `Case For Monolith`), `Coding/`, and `tools/`.
- **Output & Workflow**: The `posts/` folder is highly structured, using date-specific subfolders that further nest into specific architectural topics (e.g., `posts/2026-04-19/conway/`) to manage granular LinkedIn content drafts.
- **System & Templates**: Root-level files like `GEMINI` track high-level AI interaction context, while the `Statements/` folder contains foundational declarations and the `Agents/` folder defines interaction protocols.
- **Chronological Tracking**: The `Daily Notes/` folder and the use of date-stamped filenames (e.g., `topic-DD-MM-YYYY`) maintain a strict temporal record of learning and activity.
