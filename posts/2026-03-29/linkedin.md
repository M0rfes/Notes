🤯 **Your LLM has a massive blind spot.**

If you’re only relying on pre-trained knowledge, you’re missing the "private data" revolution. Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is the bridge, but most people are still stuck on Stage 1.

Are you using the right RAG modality for 2026?

1️⃣ **Everything as Text**: The "Legacy" approach. You turn images and audio into text summaries. It works, but you lose the nuance that only raw media can provide.

2️⃣ **Hybrid Multimodal**: The "Pragmatic" approach. You link your text to specific media files and let a Multimodal LLM (MLLM) see the "big picture." Deep context, high accuracy.

3️⃣ **Full Multimodal**: The "Cutting Edge." Text, audio, and video all live in a shared vector space. You don't just find a document; you find the exact frame in a video that answers your user's question.

RAG isn't just a search engine anymore—it’s a multimodal brain.
