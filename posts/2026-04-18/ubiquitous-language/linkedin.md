Stop acting as a translator between business and tech. 🛑

In many teams, developers spend half their time "translating" business stories into technical implementation. This is where bugs hide and velocity dies.

The solution? **Ubiquitous Language.**

It’s not just jargon. It’s a shared vocabulary that lives in the code, the stories, and the conversations between every stakeholder.

**Why it matters:**
✅ **Zero Ambiguity**: The code speaks the language of the domain experts.
✅ **Seamless Onboarding**: Any dev can pick up a story and understand the "why" without a translator.
✅ **Faster Delivery**: We stop arguing about naming and start building logic.

If your code says `UserEntity` but the business says `PolicyHolder`, you’re already losing.
