# Final Post: DDD (APPROVED)

**Your microservices are coupled because your boundaries are wrong. 🏗️**

In a distributed system, a clean API is just the beginning. To avoid the "distributed monolith," you need to hide information and eliminate translation errors between teams. This is where Domain-Driven Design (DDD) becomes your secret weapon.

**The Strategy for Better Boundaries:**

🔹 **Bounded Contexts:**
Define explicit boundaries where a specific data model is valid. By hiding internal logic from the wider organization, you give your team the freedom to refactor and deploy independently without breaking downstream consumers.

🔹 **Ubiquitous Language:**
Stop "translating" business requirements into technical jargon. If the business calls it a "Shipping Manifest," the code must reflect it. This eliminates hidden assumptions and accelerates delivery speed.

**The Business Outcome:**
When the software architecture mirrors the business domain, you reduce coupling and increase technical agility. You aren't just building software; you're mapping your organization's logic into code.
