# Final Post: Summary (APPROVED)

**There is no "perfect" way to split microservices. Only many ways to win. 🏆**

Over the past few days, we’ve explored the diverse ways to split a system into microservices. But here’s the most important lesson: the most resilient, high-velocity architectures don't just pick one strategy. They use a mix of all of them.

**The Ultimate Guide to Splitting Microservices:**

🏗️ **Domain-Driven Design (DDD):** Use Bounded Contexts to hide information and expose clean APIs. This ensures your software structure reflects your business domain.

🚀 **Volatility (Bimodal IT):** Split services based on their speed of change. Isolate high-volatility "Mode 2" innovation layers from stable "Mode 1" systems of record.

🔐 **Data Governance:** Isolate HIPAA/PCI/PII data into compliance silos to simplify audits and reduce liability.

🏢 **Conway's Law:** Align your architecture with your team structure to maximise ownership and velocity. If the organization changes, your architecture might need to as well.

**The Strategic Synthesis:**
Don't be a dogmatist. Pick the strategy that solves your current bottleneck:
- **Need faster deployments?** Look at Volatility.
- **Need better security?** Look at Data Governance.
- **Need technical clarity?** Look at DDD.
- **Need team autonomy?** Look at Conway's Law.

**The Goal:**
Build a flexible, high-velocity system that evolves with your organisation.
