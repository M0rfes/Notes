# Final Post: Volatility (APPROVED)

**Is your legacy core killing your innovation speed? 🚀**

Not all services are created equal. When splitting microservices, we often ignore the "volatility" of the business logic. If you pair a high-volatility feature with a stable "system-of-record," you create a permanent bottleneck.

**The Bimodal IT Strategy for Microservices:**

✅ **Mode 1 (The Stable Foundation):**
Focus on reliability. These are services that don't change frequently—think core accounting or user identity. They are optimised for stability, not speed.

✅ **Mode 2 (The Innovation Engine):**
Focus on speed-to-market. These services require rapid iteration and experimental features. They are optimised for rapid deployment and business innovation.

**The Golden Rule:**
Avoid the "two speeds: slow and slower" trap. If every update in your Innovation layer (Mode 2) requires a corresponding change in your Foundation (Mode 1), you haven't decoupled your system—you've just added latency to your product roadmap.

**Your Goal:**
Isolate high-volatility logic to maximise speed-to-market without compromising the stability of your core.
